Last updated: October 28, 2026

1. The service

Judo Edits ("the app") is a personal, non-commercial tool operated by its author for their own TikTok account. It creates short edited videos from judo footage and uploads them as drafts to the author's TikTok inbox using the official TikTok Content Posting API.

2. Who can use it

The app is not offered to the public. Only the author's own TikTok account is authorized to use it.

3. TikTok account access

The app only requests the user.info.basic and video.upload permissions. It never publishes content directly: every video stays a draft until the account owner reviews and publishes it in the TikTok app. Access can be revoked at any time from the TikTok app settings.

4. Content

The account owner is responsible for the videos they publish and for complying with TikTok's Community Guidelines and Terms of Service, including rights to the footage used.
