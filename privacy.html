Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

1. Summary

Judo Edits is a personal tool used only with its author's own TikTok account. It does not collect, sell or share personal data about anyone else.

2. Data processed

  • TikTok authorization tokens for the author's own account, obtained through TikTok Login Kit. They are stored encrypted in the author's private GitHub repository secrets and are used only to upload drafts.
  • Basic profile information (open ID, display name) returned by the user.info.basic permission. It is not stored.
  • Videos created by the tool and uploaded as drafts to the author's TikTok inbox.

3. What we do not do

No analytics, no cookies, no advertising, no data from other TikTok users, no sale or sharing of data with third parties.

4. Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept until access is revoked. Access can be revoked at any time in the TikTok app (Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions), which immediately stops the tool.
